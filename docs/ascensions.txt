Mortar
	Cluster Munitions
		Description: Shells split into 5 smaller bomblets midair
		Rarity: Rare
		Notes for implementation: 50% damage and area each. They should split at 2/3 of their flight path and usually land in a way where there is some overlap in the center, so it is possible for a monster to get hit by all 5 bomblets. Each bomblet can damage individually, so getting hit by multiple means taking damage multiple times.
		Additional cards: More Bomblets I-V (+1 bomblet each)
		
	Mega Mortar
		Description: How big is too big?
		Rarity: Legendary
		Notes for implementation: Adjacent Mortars in square grids (2x2, 3x3, 4x4 etc) fuse together into one big mortar, combining their experience and upgrades. Terrain should be raised to the biggest height among all tiles of the mega mortar. The explosion radius should scale with the amount of mortars fused (2x2 = 4x the radius).

Shredder
	The Saw Dimension
		Description: The floor is saw
		Rarity: Legendary
		Notes for implementation: The entire path where the monsters walk is covered in saws while this treasure is held. By default, they do 1 damage 5 times per second, but add the damage/10 of each Shredder on the map to the dps. Shredders no longer attack on their own. XP gain should be calculated centrally depending on the top-most HP layer (shield, armor or health) of all monsters on the map, e.g.: ShieldXpGain = (ShieldMonsterCount / AllMonsterCount) * ShredderCount, then divided among the shredders. It does not have to be exactly like this, but all shredders should be gaining XP as if they always had a target, but proportional to what the whole machinery is currently damaging.
