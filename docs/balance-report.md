# Balance report

Generated by the dev command `balance report` from the running game (all treasures, custom and vanilla cards, Points of Interest, towers). Copy `console/balance.md` to `docs/balance-report.md` after balance changes.

How to read it (docs/balance-framework.md): **points** ≈ % tougher monsters the item lets the board hold, at a good moment for it (the 80th percentile of its value curve over the waves it can be offered), × (situation breadth × timing breadth)^0.3. **Early / late** are the value if taken at the first offer wave / at wave 40, **peak** the best wave. **Flag** marks items outside ±25% of their rarity budget (cards: outside 2–6). Source says where the number comes from: *formula*, *estimated* (hand estimate) or a proxy marked for the M8 simulation.

Budgets: cards 2–6, Common 12, Uncommon 18, Rare 24, Epic 30, Legendary 36, Mythical 54; ascensions 25% more than their rarity; Points of Interest 15. All constants are uncalibrated until M8. The treasures are mostly placeholders: no balance changes until M9.

**Summary:** 650 items, 30 over budget, 466 under, 1 unrated, 0 missing specs.
